# Support and feature boundaries

This source candidate has no service commitment or commercial support promise.
After an approved public release, use the chosen repository's issue templates for
sanitized reproducible defects. Security reports use the approved private channel.

Hosted Quorilo Monitor accounts, subscriptions, provider connections, signed
Windows installers, Trial/Pro entitlements and experimental automation are outside
this component. No paid feature is sold or unlocked by this tool. Current product
pricing/terms remain governed by the separately accepted commercial service.
