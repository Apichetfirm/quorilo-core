# Privacy

The program reads the JSON you deliberately pipe to stdin and writes a plan to
stdout. No network calls, telemetry, account discovery, profile scanning, storage
or background service. Do not put credentials, identities, prompts or log exports
in the input. Unknown fields reject. Input is limited to16KiB and errors do not
echo it. Your shell/terminal may retain input/output or redirects; manage those
locally. No customer data or real provider fixture is included in this candidate.
