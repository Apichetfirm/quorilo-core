# Third-party inventory

No external npm packages, bundled runtimes, fonts, logos or other assets are
included in this selected source candidate. Node.js is obtained separately by
the operator under its own license/notices. No third-party MIT code is used as
an assumption that the entire Quorilo product is MIT licensed.

Owner-side provenance and publication audit must confirm rights in the selected
Quorilo module/new wrapper before the final license is applied. Adding a future
dependency requires a fresh license/notice inventory and compatible release review.
