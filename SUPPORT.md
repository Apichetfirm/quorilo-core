# Support and donations

Use public issues for sanitized reproducible software problems; use SECURITY.md
for sensitive reports. Include platform/channel/source or release identity and
expected versus observed behavior. Never share provider profiles, prompts, passwords,
cookies, full account histories or payment details. No support SLA is promised.

Donations support development voluntarily. No donation payment destination is
configured, and no donation is required for local monitoring. Future official
sponsorship must use verified owner-controlled destinations and consent for any
recognition. No fundraising total or commercial purchase is represented as a gift.

Using Free does not automatically cancel a historical subscription or request a
refund. Use the established authenticated account/support process for existing
billing obligations. Financial and legally required records are preserved.
